What the video set appears to reinforce
- The material is organized as a trading process, not isolated indicators.
- Order implied by the videos:
  - psychology / trader protection,
  - tools and contexts for good entries,
  - exits,
  - position management,
  - stops and trailing stops,
  - money management.
- This sequence matches the codebase structure already found:
  - entries/signals,
  - context filters,
  - stops/targets,
  - operational filters,
  - risk sizing / trade management.

Most likely configuration lessons reinforced by the videos
1. Entries should not be used naked.
   - Use signal + context/filter.
   - Best candidates: divergence or breakout + trend/regime filter.

2. Exits are first-class, not an afterthought.
   - The separate emphasis on exits, management, and trailing stops strongly supports using the Stop Intelligent / Mogalef Stop families as core modules.

3. Position management matters as much as entry quality.
   - Likely means testing first with one clean entry, then improving with stop/target/management before multiplying indicators.

4. Money management is a dedicated layer.
   - Confirms risk sizing should be separated from signal logic.

5. Context matters.
   - "Outils et contextes" lines up with NeutralZone, Trend Filter, XLB state, ATR Min/Max, Block Hours/Day.

Recommended research interpretation from the video set
- Build prototypes in this order:
  1) one signal,
  2) one context filter,
  3) one stop,
  4) one target or timed exit,
  5) one volatility/session filter,
  6) money management last.
